# outreach

The audience-facing side of the project: posts written to reach owners, communities and allies, as distinct from the reference docs (`docs/`) and the upstream contributions to other projects (`docs/upstream/`). These carry the same evidence, aimed at people rather than maintainers.

Everything here goes out in Daniel's own words. The files are the drafts and the record of what was posted; the raw material is his to reword and send, and each post is judged by its substance, not its volume.

## The posts

- `tabnews-post.md`, `tabnews-post-pessoal.md`, `tabnews-post-slop.md`, `tabnews-post-criterio-aplicado.md` — the TabNews essays (Brazilian Portuguese): the personal angle, the AI-slop argument, and the "criterion applied" follow-up. Written in his community voice, not the technical register of the upstream posts.
- `ltt-steamframe-forum-comment.md` — the Linus Tech Tips forum comment on the Steam Frame review (2026-09-18): its "so little 3D content" point answered as a signalling problem, not a content one, with the fix chain.
- `ltt-steamframe-followup-2026-09-26.md` — the follow-up on the same thread (2026-09-26): the movie chain closed by the mpv merge, Half-Life 2 played in 3D through Valve's own VR path, and the stereo spectator idea for the Frame, asked of Valve in SteamVR-for-Linux #961. Mentions Luke.
- `rossmann-outreach.md` — the right-to-repair outreach to Louis Rossmann / FULU, grounded in his own live Sony campaign, every quote pinned to its source.
- `discussion1-followup-samsung.md` — the follow-up on this repo's Discussion #1: the cross-brand (Samsung) confirmation that the 3D-signalling problem is not Sony-only.
- `vlc-hifi-3d-audience.md` — who publicly asked for what the VLC-HiFi and VLC-3D-HiFi builds do: the people, with links and platforms, and how to reach them.
