## What is open

### The next leg — SDL, X11, Wayland stereo signalling

This is the one Daniel named as next, and today's measurements are its foundation. The television's EDID advertises `3D present`, side-by-side half horizontal, top-and-bottom and frame packing on VICs 5, 20, 34, 60 and 62. The Linux kernel has carried `DRM_MODE_FLAG_3D_*` and `DRM_CLIENT_CAP_STEREO_3D` for over a decade. The connector still exposes 30 modes and no stereo mode, because nothing in the desktop stack sets that client capability. Capability at both ends, no layer in between.

Before filing anything: verify by running, per project doctrine. Do not claim an upstream absence from code search alone. Fetch the sources, build, and demonstrate it.

## Conventions that keep this work credible

Evidence is tagged [proven], [qualified] or [inferred] and the tags are honest. Several claims were published this week from a partner model's report without checking and turned out wrong: RPCS3 shipped SBS in April 2023, the Nestopia issue closed in January 2016, Dolphin amplifies primitives in a single geometry-shader pass rather than rendering twice. Each was one API call away from being checked. Check before publishing, and when something published turns out wrong, correct it in the repo with a commit that says so.

When Daniel contradicts a model on stereoscopy, verify rather than hedge. His hit rate in this domain is high and the models' is not. The SEI discovery that started this whole project came from his question, not from code reading.
